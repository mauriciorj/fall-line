Frequently Asked Ticket Questions

Are Lift Tickets Refundable?

Lift tickets are eligible for a partial refund or resort credit if notice is provided to Lakeridge Resort by the refund deadline of 12:00pm the day prior to your scheduled activity. Refund requests received after this deadline will not be eligible for a refund, resort credit or date/time change.  There are no exceptions.

Same day purchases are final sale, no exceptions

Please see refund options below.

All refund requests must be made by email to  admin@lakeridgeresort.ca.   Please include your order number and specify the refund option you are requesting.   Review options below.

OPTION #1:  Partial Refund

A refund minus a 15% administration fee, issued to the same method of payment used for the original purchase.

OPTION #2:  Resort Credit

A 100% refund issued as resort credit which can be applied to future purchases.  Resort credit does not expire.

NOTE:

  • Refund requests received after 12pm the day prior to your scheduled activity will NOT be eligible for a refund, resort credit or date/time change.
  • We do not guarantee snow conditions, weather conditions, open terrain or number of lifts operating.
  • Please allow up to 2 business days for your refund to be processed.

Do I need to make a reservation for lift tickets?

Advance reservations for lift tickets are highly recommended, especially during peak times. You can book your tickets online to guarantee availability.

If I am only going on the Magic Carpet hill do I need to purchase a lift ticket?

A lift ticket is required to have access to any of the slopes at Lakeridge Resort, including the Magic Carpet hill. A ‘Magic Carpet Only ticket’ is available if you wish to only use the magic carpet (beginner) area during your visit.  You MUST have either ski boots or snowboard boots on to step foot on our hills.

What happens if I lose my lift ticket?

If you lose your lift ticket, a new one will need to be purchased. Please ensure your lift tickets are attached securely to your jacket with a supplied fresh wire hanger, avoid placing new tickets atop old tickets as they may not adhere properly in cold weather.

I purchased my ticket for the wrong date, can I make a date change?

Please email admin@lakeridgeresort.ca for assistance in changing the date. Note, that any difference in price will need to be paid.

I purchased my tickets online, how do I get my tickets?

Simply show your order confirmation or barcode to one of our Customer Service Representatives inside the chalet to pick up your tickets. Please enter through the southwest doors.

I did not receive an order confirmation/barcode. What do I do?

The order confirmation/barcode will be sent to the email provided during your order. Please check your junk/spam mail folder. If you have still not received this information please visit Guest Services located in the Chalet.

Do I need to have Ski or Snowboard Equipment on when teaching someone?

To access the hill you must have a valid lift ticket.  Ski Boots or Snowboard Boots are mandatory.  You can not wear regular winter boots on any of the ski hills.

Does a lift ticket and season pass include snow tubing?

No, snow tubing tickets must be purchased separately.

Can I Ski or Snowboard before my Night Pass is Valid?

You may purchase a 1, 2, or 3-hour lift ticket before your night pass becomes valid. This option is available to night pass holders only
